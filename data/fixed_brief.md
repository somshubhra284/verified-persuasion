# Do you need a health insurance policy of your own?

Oct 15, 2026 · @Som

Many working Indians rely on employer cover, a parent's policy, savings or a government scheme instead of a policy in their own name. This page sets out what official data says about each of those. It does not recommend any product and is not financial advice. Each fact carries a tag such as [C02] that links it to a named source at the end.

## How often people end up in hospital

- In 2025 there were about 3 hospital admissions a year for every 100 urban residents (all ages, excluding childbirth). [C01]
- In 2025, 44.3% of urban residents had any health insurance or scheme cover, so more than half had none. [C28]
- Nearly two in three urban hospital stays (excluding childbirth) happen in private hospitals, including government-empanelled ones, according to the same survey's full report. [C03]
- Two in three people killed on Indian roads in 2023 were aged 18 to 45. [C04]
- About 1 in 9 Indian adults aged 20 and over has diabetes, and more than 1 in 3 has high blood pressure. [C05]

## What a hospital stay costs

- In 2025, the typical (median) private-hospital stay cost ₹24,000 (≈$248) out of pocket, excluding childbirth; the average was ₹50,508 (≈$522). [C02]
- In 2022–23, households paid about 43% of all health spending in India directly out of pocket. [C18]
- Even the government's negotiated rate for an angioplasty is ₹50,800 (≈$525) plus the stent; private hospitals aren't bound by these rates. [C33]
- Employer medical plan costs in India are projected to rise 11.5% in 2026, after 13% in 2025. [C17]
- The average health claim paid in 2024–25 was about ₹28,900 (≈$299), according to the regulator's annual report. [C21]

## If your employer covers you

- Employer cover belongs to the employer's group policy. The regulator's option to move it into your own name applies only when you leave the group, which means the cover is tied to the job. [C06]
- Moving from group cover to your own policy may be subject to the insurer's underwriting. [C07]
- If you migrate without a break in cover, time served under the group policy counts toward waiting periods. [C09]
- The rules carry waiting-period credit over only by migration or portability, which means a fresh policy bought after leaving a job starts its waiting periods from zero. [C08]
- According to a 2023 survey by the insurtech Plum, the median employer health cover is about ₹3 lakh (≈$3,100). [C26]
- Of about 58 crore lives covered by health insurance in 2024–25, only about 6 crore were under individually bought policies, according to the regulator's annual report; 27.5 crore were on group plans. [C27]

## If you're on a parent's policy

- According to an insurer-sponsored article, children are usually covered on a parent's family policy only until about 25, or until they marry. [C32]

## If you plan to buy later

- The waiting period for a pre-existing condition you declare can be at most 3 years. [C11]
- There is no longer a regulatory maximum age to buy health insurance, though an insurer can still decline an applicant. [C15]
- Since Jan 2025, the regulator has capped yearly premium increases for policyholders over 60 at 10%. [C16]

## If you worry claims get rejected

- Of about 3.7 crore health claims handled in 2024–25, 87% were paid, 8% were rejected and about 5% were still pending at year end, according to the regulator's annual report. [C10]
- The government says health claims are usually rejected under the policy's exclusion clauses, or because the treatment didn't need hospital admission. [C29]
- After 5 years of continuous cover, an insurer can't reject a claim for non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except established fraud; policy exclusions still apply. [C12]
- Insurers must decide cashless requests within 1 hour; from Aug 2024 to May 2025, 87% met that. [C14]
- In 2024–25, about 4 in 10 health complaints taken to the Insurance Ombudsman were resolved in the policyholder's favour, as reported by Cafemutual. [C13]
- When the Ombudsman issued a formal award on a health complaint in 2023–24, about 6 in 10 went for the policyholder. [C30]

## What cover costs now

- Individual health insurance premiums carry no GST since 22 Sep 2025; employer group policies still pay 18%. [C19]
- The tax deduction on premiums (up to ₹25,000, ≈$258, below age 60) applies only under the old tax regime; the new regime is the default. [C20]

## If you count on Ayushman Bharat

- Ayushman Bharat's base cover goes to families selected mainly from the SECC 2011 deprivation and occupation lists; it is not open to anyone who applies. [C22]
- The base scheme covers over 12 crore families. [C24]
- Ayushman Bharat covers everyone regardless of income only from age 70. [C23]
