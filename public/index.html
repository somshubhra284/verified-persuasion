Verified Persuasion

Can a chatbot that only states facts it can source change a belief, as well as a plain information sheet can?

This is a small study for the 100xEngineers capstone. It looks at one belief many working Indians hold: "I don't need a health insurance policy of my own."

The chatbot answers your reason using only claims from a checked list of official sources, and says "I don't have a source for that" when it can't back something up. It takes about 10 minutes.
